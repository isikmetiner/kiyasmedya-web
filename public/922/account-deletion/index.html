922 — Account Deletion

App: 922  ·  Developer: KIYAS MEDYA

You can permanently delete your 922 account and all of your data on our server from this page or from inside the app. Deletion cannot be undone.

Delete from this page

Continue with the email address and password you use to sign in to the app.

Are you sure?

Your account has been deleted

Your account and all of your data on our server have been deleted. If you keep using the app on your phone you will be signed out; you can continue playing without an account if you like.

Deleting from inside the app

In the 922 app, go to Profil → Hesap → Hesabı sil (Profile → Account → Delete account; the app is in Turkish) and enter your password. Your account is deleted immediately.

If you don't remember your password

You can set a new password with Şifremi unuttum (Forgot password) on the sign-in screen of the app, or send an email from your account's address to contact@kiyasmedya.com with the subject “922 account deletion”. We fulfil emailed requests and reply within 30 days. Use this option for accounts whose email address was never verified as well.

Data that is deleted

  • Your email address, display name, player code and password hash
  • Your rank avatar choice and hometown
  • Your game records and your answers in ranked games
  • Your ratings (Elo, Kıyas Points), rank, streaks and season results
  • Your duels and duel answers
  • Your friendships and pending friend requests
  • Reports you made and reports about you, and your blocks

Once your account is deleted, your name no longer appears on the leaderboards or in other players' duel and friend lists.

Data that is kept, and for how long

Your data remains in the server's nightly backups for at most 14 days; after that the backups are deleted too. Backups are only used to restore the database after a failure.

Records and settings kept on your phone while playing without an account are never sent to our server; they are removed from your phone when you delete the app.

Advertising data is collected by Google and is not kept on our server; see the Advertising section of the privacy policy.

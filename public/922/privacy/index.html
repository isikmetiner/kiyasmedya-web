922 — Privacy Policy

App: 922  ·  Developer: KIYAS MEDYA

922 does not require an account to play. The app shows ads; the ads are served by Google AdMob, and for this some information about your device is sent to Google. This policy explains which information reaches whom, what is stored if you create an account, and how you can change your choices.

Data we collect

When you play without an account, none of your personal data reaches KIYAS MEDYA. The ads in the app, however, are served by Google, and Google collects information about your device for this purpose; see the Ads section below for details.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress is stored primarily on your own device:

  • Number of games played and total number of correct answers
  • Your highest score for each game mode and difficulty level
  • A record of your recent games (mode, difficulty, score, date)
  • Your in-app preferences (for example, haptic feedback)
  • A randomly generated, device-specific anonymous identifier

This anonymous identifier is not linked to any personal information and does not identify you. If you delete the app, all of this data is removed from your device.

If you create an account

An account is needed to back up your scores, appear on the leaderboard, add friends and play duels, and it is optional. Only if you choose to create one is the following information stored on our server:

  • Your email address — to verify your account and let you sign in
  • Your display name — shown on the leaderboard, to your friends and to your duel opponents
  • Your player code — a short identifier you can share so others can add you as a friend
  • Your password — stored in an irreversibly hashed form; it is never kept in plain text anywhere
  • Your game records — mode, difficulty, score and time played
  • Your friends list — who you are friends with
  • Your duel records — who you played against, the scores, and the answer you gave and the joker you used on each question; once a duel is over, your opponent can see the question-by-question breakdown

Your email address is never shown to other users, on the leaderboard or anywhere else. Other players see only your display name, your score and your answers in duels you played together.

This data is used to operate the game: managing your account, storing your scores, the leaderboard, friends and duels. Your account data is not used for advertising and is not sent to Google. We do not sell your data.

Service providers

We work with a small number of providers to deliver the service. They process your data only on our behalf and only to provide this service:

  • Server hosting — the server where account and game records are kept
  • Email delivery — your email address passes through the delivery service so that your verification code can reach you
  • Google AdMob — serving ads; what information it receives is described in the Ads section below

Apart from these, we do not transfer your account data to third parties. Should a legal obligation arise, we act within the framework of the applicable legislation.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and all of your game records on the server by following Profile → Delete account in the app. This cannot be undone, and you do not need to request anything from us.

Location

The app shows a map of Türkiye but does not access your location. The map data is bundled inside the app; your device's location services are not used in any way.

Permissions

The app does not ask you for any permission: no access is requested to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, files or notifications. On Android, internet access and access to the advertising ID are used for ads; these are system permissions that do not require a consent prompt.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children. Creating an account is optional. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Ads

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: banner ads on the main menu and at the end of a game, plus optional rewarded ads (ads you choose to watch in exchange for a joker, a hint or removing a mistake).

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect the following information:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID)
  • Your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (city/country level)
  • Technical information such as device model, operating system, language and app version
  • Your interactions with ads (impressions, clicks) and error/performance logs

This information is used to serve ads, measure ads and prevent fraud; where you have given consent, it may also be used to personalize ads. How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices: In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your ad consent on first launch. In some US states you can object to the sale or sharing of your data. In these regions you can change your decision at any time via Profile → Ad privacy options. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads. On iOS the app does not ask for permission to track you and does not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

The app does not use third-party analytics tools.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published at this address and the date above will be updated. If there is a material change in how your data is collected or used, you will be notified in the app before the change takes effect.

Language

This policy is also available in Turkish. In case of any discrepancy between the two versions, the Turkish version prevails.

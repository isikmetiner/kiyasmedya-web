922 — Privacy Policy

App: 922  ·  Developer: KIYAS MEDYA

922 does not require an account to play. If you use it without an account, none of your personal data reaches us. This policy explains what information is stored, and how it is used, if you choose to create an account.

Data we collect

No data is collected in order to play the game. You can download the app and play without an account; in that case none of your information reaches us.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress is stored primarily on your own device:

  • Number of games played and total number of correct answers
  • Your highest score for each game mode and difficulty level
  • A record of your recent games (mode, difficulty, score, date)
  • Your in-app preferences (for example, haptic feedback)
  • A randomly generated, device-specific anonymous identifier

This anonymous identifier is not linked to any personal information and does not identify you. If you delete the app, all of this data is removed from your device.

If you create an account

Backing up your scores and appearing on the leaderboard is optional. Only if you choose to do so is the following information stored on our server:

  • Your email address — to verify your account and let you sign in
  • Your display name — shown on the leaderboard
  • Your password — stored in an irreversibly hashed form; it is never kept in plain text anywhere
  • Your game records — mode, difficulty, score and time played

Your email address is never shown to other users, on the leaderboard or anywhere else. Other players see only your display name and your score.

This data is used to operate the game: managing your account, storing your scores and building the leaderboard. We do not sell your data.

Service providers

We work with a small number of providers to deliver the service. They process your data only on our behalf and only to provide this service:

  • Server hosting — the server where account and game records are kept
  • Email delivery — your email address passes through the delivery service so that your verification code can reach you

Apart from this, we do not transfer your data to third parties. Should a legal obligation arise, we act within the framework of the applicable legislation.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and all of your game records on the server by following Profile → Delete account in the app. This cannot be undone, and you do not need to request anything from us.

Location

The app shows a map of Türkiye but does not access your location. The map data is bundled inside the app; your device's location services are not used in any way.

Permissions

The app requests no device permissions. No access is requested to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, files or notifications.

Children's privacy

The app is not specifically directed at children. Creating an account is optional; no personal data is collected when the app is used without an account.

Advertising and analytics

This version of the app does not show ads and does not use third-party analytics tools.

Advertising or analytics services may be added in the future. In that case: the data these services access will be stated clearly in this policy, the policy will be updated before the change takes effect, and our data declarations in the app stores will be updated accordingly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published at this address and the date above will be updated. If there is a material change in how your data is collected or used, you will be notified in the app before the change takes effect.

Language

This policy is also available in Turkish. In case of any discrepancy between the two versions, the Turkish version prevails.

Contact

For questions: contact@kiyasmedya.com
